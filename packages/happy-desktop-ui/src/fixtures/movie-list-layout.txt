I left off the movies every one of these threads already assumes you've seen: *Die Hard*, *John Wick*, *The Matrix*, *Alien*, *The Thing*, *Hereditary*, *The Shining*. These 20 are pulled from different X lists and lean toward stuff a typical action/horror/thriller queue still misses.

**Horror that doesn't get watched as often as it should**

1. **The Descent** (2005). Cave survival that turns into a monster movie. From [@alexharpermodel](https://x.com/alexharpermodel/status/2108727417271644234)'s recs and [@JorCru](https://x.com/JorCru/status/2110100558610874816)'s action-horror list. Genuinely stressful.
2. **Barbarian** (2022). Airbnb horror that keeps changing shape. From the [31-night marathon thread](https://x.com/infinitycollar/status/2102171330200105221). Go in cold.
3. **Ready or Not** (2019). Wedding-night hide-and-seek with a rich family that hunts. Same marathon thread. Mean, funny, short.
4. **Late Night with the Devil** (2023). A 1970s talk show slowly goes to hell on live TV. Same thread. Easy watch, sticks with you.
5. **Audition** (1999). Japanese. Starts like a sad dating movie. Does not stay that way. From [@lonerpotterhead](https://x.com/lonerpotterhead/status/2110055585374953537)'s categorized list.
6. **Noroi: The Curse** (2005). Japanese fake documentary. Same list's found-footage picks. Slow, and a lot of people call it the best one most folks never put on.
7. **As Above, So Below** (2014). Found footage in the Paris catacombs. From the alexharpermodel list. Dumb-fun that actually works.
8. **Incantation** (2022). Taiwanese found footage. From [@Pasia_thegoat](https://x.com/Pasia_thegoat/status/2110573025519403039). Cursed-video energy, very effective if you let it.
9. **Near Dark** (1987). Kathryn Bigelow vampire western. Came up in [@BeAfraid_Movies](https://x.com/BeAfraid_Movies/status/2026860992789962849)'s pre-2000 community vote. Cooler than most vampire movies.
10. **Trick 'r Treat** (2007). Halloween anthology. From [@reIeasethebats](https://x.com/reIeasethebats/status/2109435595789414561)'s seasonal thread. Cult for a reason. Best in October.

**Action and thrillers off the main canon**

11. **Hard Boiled** (1992). John Woo, Chow Yun-fat, hospital shootout. Collider keeps parking it next to *Die Hard* and *Speed* as pure entertainment ([one of those posts](https://x.com/Collider/status/2060481478144766037)). If you like John Wick, this is the grandparent.
12. **Under Siege** (1992). *Die Hard* on a battleship, and Seagal's one actually good movie. From the [80s/90s action roundup](https://x.com/theRealEnzoMac/status/2094073461333246088).
13. **The Kingdom** (2007). FBI team in Saudi Arabia, investigation that becomes a firefight. [@Cele__Audu](https://x.com/Cele__Audu/status/2109252948094046690) singled it out. Berg directing, feels like a lost Michael Mann cousin.
14. **The Killer** (2023). Fincher's assassin procedural. From the ["action, spy & assassin thrillers you must watch"](https://x.com/tweetsbyzyl/status/2074745445851529386) post. Cold, precise, funny if you like process.
15. **A Bittersweet Life** (2005). Korean. Stylish gangster revenge, Lee Byung-hun. From the [Korean action/thriller thread](https://x.com/jackofficial_01/status/2092673332336267367).
16. **The Yellow Sea** (2010). Korean. Same thread. A broke cab driver takes a hit job and everything goes rotten. Brutal, long, great.
17. **The Villainess** (2017). Korean. Same thread. Opens with a first-person hallway massacre. The rest is a spy melodrama with insane set pieces.

**The overlap: horror that moves like an action movie**

18. **Green Room** (2015). Punk band vs. neo-Nazis in a bar. [@JorCru](https://x.com/JorCru/status/2110100558610874816)'s action-horror picks. 90 minutes, no fat, Patrick Stewart as a villain.
19. **VFW** (2019). Same list. Old veterans hold a bar against junkie mutants. Pure guy-movie, barely anyone saw it.
20. **The Wailing** (2016). Korean. From both the [Korean horror starter set](https://x.com/Mclipssz/status/2094145636559904925) and Pasia's slow-burn recs. Cop, village, shaman, something evil. Two and a half hours and it earns it.

If you only do five and want the "I probably haven't seen this" cut: **Noroi**, **The Descent**, **Hard Boiled**, **Green Room**, **The Wailing**.
